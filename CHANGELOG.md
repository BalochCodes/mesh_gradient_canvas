## 0.0.1

* Initial release of `mesh_gradient_canvas`.
* Organic, multi-color mesh gradient canvas inspired by Stripe and iOS 18.
* High-performance 60/120 FPS CustomPainter rendering with multi-point radial blur blending.
* Multi-blob organic floating physics with customizable speed and amplitude.
* Interactive touch reactivity: drag anchors or tap to create ripple distortions.
* Prebuilt factory palettes: Stripe Aurora, iOS Sunset, Cyber Neon, Cotton Candy, and Dark Oasis.
* Programmatic `MeshGradientController` for runtime play, pause, speed adjustment, and point manipulation.
